It fills forms for you.

fill.ai reads your resume and remembers you. On any form, press Alt Shift F or click the fill.ai icon. It fills what it knows, asks about what it doesn't, and never presses submit.

1

Choose the AI

It reads your resume once, then answers each form. Two of the three are free. You can switch any time.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. No card needed. It stays in this browser.

On the free tier, Google may use what fill.ai sends, your resume included, to improve its products. If that matters to you, choose "On this computer".

Get a key at console.anthropic.com. API use is billed by Anthropic, separately from a Claude subscription. It stays in this browser.
