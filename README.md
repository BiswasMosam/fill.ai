# fill.ai

**Forms, filled by an AI that actually knows you.**

Job applications, college portals, Google Forms, visa paperwork: every one asks the same things again, in a slightly different layout. fill.ai learns who you are once, from your resume, portfolio or anything else you give it, and fills forms for you from then on.

> **Status: design phase.** Nothing is built yet. This README describes what fill.ai is meant to do.

## How it will work

1. **Teach it once.** Give it your resume, portfolio or LinkedIn export. It reads them and builds a structured profile: education, experience, projects, skills, links, contact details.
2. **Open any form.** A small side panel sits in the corner of the screen. You can drag it anywhere so it never covers the form.
3. **It reads the form.** fill.ai works out what each field is asking for and matches it against your profile.
4. **It shows you what it knows and what it doesn't.** Fields it can answer are filled in for you to check. For anything missing you choose: type it in just this once, or save it to your profile so you're never asked again.
5. **You submit.** fill.ai never presses submit for you.

## Principles

- **It never makes things up.** Every answer comes from something you gave it. If it doesn't know, it asks.
- **You stay in control.** Nothing is submitted without you, and anything the AI writes for an open question is clearly marked as a draft.
- **Your data is yours.** Your profile is readable, editable and deletable at any time.
